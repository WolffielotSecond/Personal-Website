- Press **WSAD** to move.
- Press **E** to interact.
- Press **Space** to parry attacks.
- Press **F** to melee the zombie when it is stunned.
- Press **Q** to heal.
- Hold the **Right Mouse Button** to aim. While aiming, press the **Left Mouse Button** to shoot and **R** to reload.
- Press **C** to crouch.
