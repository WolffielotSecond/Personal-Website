**The Awakening Legacy** is an early playable prototype of **The Awakening** and the original version of the project.

Developed using **Unreal Engine 5.6** and primarily **Blueprint Visual Scripting**, the project was originally created as a course assignment for **GDIM 61**.

Due to the limited development time and scope of the early project, Legacy featured simplified gameplay systems and design structures while introducing many preliminary concepts that would later form the foundation of the full game. As the project's first experimental iteration, Legacy served as an initial exploration of gameplay design, technical implementation, and the overall development direction.

As the project's scope and design ambitions continued to grow, the original technical architecture and gameplay systems could no longer meet the requirements of the full game. Consequently, development shifted toward a complete overhaul, rebuilding the core systems from the ground up using a combination of **Unreal C++ and Blueprint Visual Scripting**, while significantly expanding the game's content.

Development of **The Awakening Legacy** has officially ended, and its original design and technical implementation have been superseded by the completely rebuilt version of **The Awakening**, which is currently in active development.
