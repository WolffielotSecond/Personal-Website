**The Awakening** is a semi-linear, 2.5D side-scrolling adventure game centered on psychological exploration and emotional storytelling. The game combines environmental exploration, puzzle-solving, parkour, light combat, and narrative-driven gameplay.

The story revolves around two sisters. After the younger sister attempts to take her own life following prolonged psychological distress, the older sister unexpectedly finds herself transported into her younger sister's inner world. Shaped by memories, emotions, and the subconscious, this world contains both familiar environments from reality and distorted landscapes filled with unknown creatures and inexplicable phenomena.

Players take on the role of the older sister as she explores this strange yet eerily familiar world. By navigating dangerous environments, solving puzzles, and uncovering hidden clues, players will gradually piece together the younger sister's past, come to understand the suffering she has endured, and unravel the mysteries behind this world.

As the journey progresses, the boundary between reality and imagination begins to blur. Will the older sister be able to find her younger sister and awaken her from the depths of her unconscious mind?

The project is developed using **Unreal Engine 5.8**, combining **Unreal C++** and **Blueprint Visual Scripting** to implement core gameplay systems, alongside a **JSON-based localization system** and **Audiokinetic Wwise audio middleware**.

The game is planned for release on **Windows PC** and **macOS**, with support for keyboard and mouse, Xbox controllers, and PlayStation DualSense® wireless controllers.

**The Awakening** is currently in active development, with its official announcement and release tentatively scheduled for **2029**.
