**The House of Janus** is a pixel-art, 2D top-down exploration and extraction game. Players take on the role of an adventurer entering a mysterious house that exists across two interconnected dimensions: the Outer World and the Inner World. By traveling between these two realms, players must search for supplies and experimental records left behind in the Inner World, bring them back to the Outer World, and ultimately escape the house. Throughout their exploration, players must carefully manage the noise generated by their footsteps to avoid alerting lurking monsters and minimize the risks of exploration and extraction.

The game centers around a dual-world switching mechanic, requiring players to navigate between the Outer World and the Inner World, explore unknown areas, collect valuable resources, and balance potential rewards against the dangers they may encounter.

The project was developed using **Unity 2022.3**, with **WebGL** as its target platform and support for keyboard and mouse controls.

**The House of Janus** was created as an entry for **Winter 2026 ZOTJAM**, a game jam organized by the **UCI Video Game Development Club (VGDC)**. The event challenged participants to develop a game within **48 hours** based on the theme **"Blink"**. Designed around this theme, the project was developed into a playable prototype within the limited timeframe of the competition.
