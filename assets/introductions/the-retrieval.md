**The Retrieval** is a 3D fixed-camera survival horror game focused on exploration, puzzle-solving, and combat, inspired by classic survival horror titles. Players take on the role of an agent infiltrating a zombie-infested mansion to locate and recover a mysterious artifact hidden within, before attempting to escape safely.

The game draws significant inspiration from the early **Resident Evil** series, featuring classic fixed-camera perspectives alongside gameplay mechanics such as key-based puzzles, resource management, and melee parrying. Players must explore the mansion, locate essential items, unlock new areas, and carefully manage their limited resources and weapons to survive the threats lurking within.

The project was developed using **Unity 2022.3**, with **WebGL** as its target platform and support for keyboard and mouse controls.

**The Retrieval** was developed as an individual project for **GDIM 33**, a course assignment focused on creating a 3D horror game. The project explores Unity's core development tools and systems, incorporating **Unity Visual Scripting, Shader Graph, Finite State Machines (FSMs), Animator, Prefabs,** and **ScriptableObjects** to implement fundamental gameplay interactions, character animations, enemy behaviors, and environmental visuals.

As a coursework project primarily focused on technical experimentation, **The Retrieval** applies the design principles of classic survival horror games to integrate multiple Unity development tools into a playable, interactive 3D environment.
