Controls Guide

  • Press WSAD to move.
  • Press E to interact.
  • Press Space to parry attacks.
  • Press F to melee the zombie when it is stunned.
  • Press Q to heal.
  • Press Right Mouse Button to aim and while aiming press Left Mouse Button to shoot and R to reload.
  • Press C to crouch.
  • Press WSAD to move.
  • Press Space to blink.
  • Use the Mouse Wheel to change speed.
  • Press Tab to open the inventory.
  • Press 1 to use a medpack. Each medpack restores one point of health.
  • Press 2 to use an accelerator. It briefly increases your speed to help you escape.
